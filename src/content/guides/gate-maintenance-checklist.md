## Safety first

Before working near an automated gate, switch the operator off or disconnect it using the manual release, so the gate cannot move while your hands are in it. Read your operator's manual. Where it differs from this list, follow the manual.

## Every month: test the safety devices

Use the test method in your operator manual. In general terms:

- [ ] **Photo eyes.** With the gate closing, block the beam with an object. The gate should stop or reverse.
- [ ] **Edge sensors.** Press the edge while the gate is moving. The gate should stop or reverse.
- [ ] **Obstruction sensing.** Follow the manual's procedure for testing inherent reversing.
- [ ] **Clean the lenses** on photo eyes and check that they are aligned and firmly mounted.

If any test fails, stop using the automatic function and call for service. Do not bypass a safety device.

## Spring

### Structure

- [ ] Sight along the top of the gate. Is it still level? Do double leaves still meet evenly?
- [ ] Look at the posts. Any lean, any cracks in the concrete, any gap opening at the base?
- [ ] Check welds at hinges, braces and corners for cracks or rust lines.
- [ ] Check that stops, latches and drop rods engage fully.

### Moving parts

- [ ] Grease hinge fittings. Wipe off the excess.
- [ ] On sliding gates, check rollers and guides for wear, and clear debris from the path.
- [ ] Disconnect the operator and move the gate by hand. It should move freely and stay where you leave it. If it binds or drifts, note where.

### Finish

- [ ] Wash off road grit and de-icer with water and mild soap.
- [ ] Look for chips and scratches, especially along the bottom rail.
- [ ] Touch up bare spots with a matching paint after removing any rust.
- [ ] Clear the drain holes at the bottom of tubes.

### Automation

- [ ] Open the operator cover. Look for insects, nests, moisture and corrosion.
- [ ] Check that wiring is secure and conduit seals are intact.
- [ ] Test the battery by cycling the gate with utility power off.
- [ ] Test every remote, the keypad and the intercom.
- [ ] On solar systems, clean the panel and check that nothing has grown up to shade it.

## Fall

Repeat the spring list, then prepare for winter:

- [ ] Replace a weak battery now.
- [ ] Confirm cold-weather lubricant where the manufacturer calls for it.
- [ ] Trim vegetation away from the gate, the operator and the sensors.
- [ ] Mark the driveway edges, loops and low devices for the plow.
- [ ] Decide where plowed snow will be stored, clear of the gate's path.
- [ ] Check that you know where the manual release is and that it works.

Our guide to [gates in snow and ice](/resources/gates-in-snow-and-ice/) covers winter operation.

## What to leave to a technician

- Adjusting limits, force or timing.
- Anything inside the control board.
- Replacing operator components.
- Re-hanging a gate or resetting a post.
- Adding or relocating safety devices.

## Signs you should call

- The gate has started to drag, sag or bind.
- The operator stalls, reverses or sounds different.
- A safety device fails its test.
- You see a crack in a weld or a post leaning.
- Water is getting into the operator.
