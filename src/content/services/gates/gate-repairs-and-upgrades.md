## Start with what is actually wrong

A symptom is not a diagnosis. "The gate will not close" has at least six causes, and replacing the operator fixes only one of them. We look at the gate as a system.

| Symptom | Common causes |
| --- | --- |
| Leaf drags or sags | Worn hinges, hinge post leaning, frame out of square |
| Leaves do not meet | Post movement, bent frame, loose stop |
| Slide gate binds | Worn rollers, debris, misaligned receiver, track damage |
| Operator stalls or reverses | Binding gate, weak battery, sensor fault, cold grease |
| Opens but will not close | Blocked or misaligned photo eye, loop fault |
| Dead, no response | Power, fuse, board, wiring or a tripped breaker |

## Structural and mechanical repair

Because we fabricate, we can fix steel properly. That means cutting out and replacing a rotted bottom rail, adding a brace to a leaf that was never stiff enough, re-welding a cracked hinge mount, or building a new leaf to match an old one. A post that has moved gets reset on a proper footing, not shimmed.

## Operators and electrical

We troubleshoot limits, boards, batteries, wiring and accessories on swing and slide operators. If a unit is worth repairing, we repair it. If it is at the end of its life or parts are gone, we recommend a replacement sized for the gate, and reuse sound wiring and devices where that makes sense. See [gate operators](/automation/gate-operators/).

## Safety upgrades

Gate operator safety standards have tightened over the years. Current editions of UL 325 call for monitored entrapment protection devices, which older installations frequently lack. If your automated gate has no photo eyes, or has devices that the operator does not monitor, it is worth a look. We explain the requirements under [safety and compliance](/automation/safety-and-compliance/).

## Upgrades worth making

- **Add automation** to a sound manual gate.
- **Add a keypad, intercom or app control** to an existing operator.
- **Add battery backup** so an outage does not trap you in or out.
- **Convert to solar** where the utility run is unreliable or far away.
- **Refinish.** Strip, repair and recoat a gate that is structurally sound but tired.
