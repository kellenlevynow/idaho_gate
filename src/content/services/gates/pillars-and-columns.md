## Why pillars and gates should be designed together

Most pillar problems come from sequence. The mason builds two handsome columns. Then the gate arrives and there is nowhere to mount a hinge that will hold, the operator arm hits the stone, and the conduit for the keypad has to be surface-run up a finished face.

Drawing the pillars with the gate avoids all three.

- **Position.** Pillar faces are set so hinges, stops and operator arms clear the stone through the full swing.
- **Structure.** The load path for the gate is decided first, then dressed.
- **Services.** Conduit for power, lighting and low-voltage devices is cast into the core before any stone goes on.
- **Proportion.** Pillar width, height and cap are sized to the gate, so neither looks like an afterthought.

The elevation on this page shows a concept estate gate between pillars, with separate steel hinge posts carrying the leaves.

## How we build them

1. **Footing.** Excavated below local frost depth and reinforced. Larger where the pillar carries gate load.
2. **Core.** Reinforced block or formed concrete, filled solid where steel is embedded.
3. **Conduit and boxes.** Placed for lanterns, address lighting, keypads and intercoms.
4. **Veneer.** Stone or brick laid with proper ties and joints.
5. **Cap.** Sloped and overhanging so water drips clear of the face.

Masonry is a trade of its own. Where a project calls for it we bring in experienced masons and coordinate their work with our steel, so you have one schedule and one point of contact.

## Choosing a material

Look at the house first. If it has stone, match or complement it. If it is timber and glass, weathering steel or concrete columns may suit it better than rock. On rural properties, stone that looks like it came off the land usually reads best.

## Finishing touches

Pillars are the natural home for the details that finish an entrance: a cut steel [address plate](/gates/address-plates-and-lighting/), a lantern on each cap, a recessed keypad, a mail or parcel box. Decide on them early and they can be built in instead of bolted on.
