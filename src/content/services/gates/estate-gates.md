## The entrance sets the tone for the whole property

On a lake estate or a long acreage drive, the gate is the first built thing a visitor sees. It tells them what to expect from everything behind it. A gate that looks ordered from a catalog undercuts a custom home. A gate that picks up the home's lines, materials and setting makes the property feel finished from the road.

That is the brief for an estate gate: design it as part of the architecture, then build it like a structure.

## Designed as one system

A large entrance has more moving parts than people expect. We draw them together so nothing gets solved late.

- **Gate leaves.** Size, weight and wind area set everything downstream.
- **Hinge posts and footings.** Sized to the leaf, set below frost depth.
- **Pillars.** Located so the hinges, operators and swing all clear the stone.
- **Operators.** Chosen for the real leaf weight and cycle count, not the brochure.
- **Access devices.** Keypad pedestal, intercom, photo eyes, vehicle loops.
- **Power and lighting.** Conduit routes, transformer location, lantern wiring.

The exploded view on our [home page](/) shows how those parts relate on a concept gate.

## Feature metalwork

This is where a custom shop earns its place. We cut panels on a CNC plasma table from our own drawings, so a ridgeline can be your ridgeline. Layering two plates with a standoff gap gives real depth and a shadow line that changes through the day. Frames can carry forged collars, hammered bar or clean modern reveals. See [decorative panels and screens](/fabrication/privacy-screens-and-decorative-panels/) for more on cut work.

## Structure you do not see

A wide leaf acts like a sail and a lever at the same time. We use heavier wall tube in the hinge stile, add internal bracing where infill will hide it, and size hinges with margin and grease fittings. Solid or semi-solid designs get a wind check, because a gate that an operator can move on a calm day can stall or slam in a prairie gust.

Where loads or local rules call for stamped calculations, we work with a licensed engineer. See [design and engineering](/company/design-engineering/).
