## How a swing gate carries its load

Stand a ladder against a wall and hang a bucket off the far end. That is a gate leaf. All of the weight wants to rotate the hinge side out of the ground and drop the latch side. Everything about a good swing gate is aimed at resisting that one force.

- **The frame** stays flat because the hinge stile is heavy and the leaf is braced.
- **The hinges** are sized for the weight with margin and can be greased.
- **The post** is stiff enough not to deflect, with a footing deep and wide enough not to rotate.

Get those three right and the latch lines up for decades.

## What the site has to give you

Draw the arc of each leaf on the ground. Everything inside it has to stay clear and close to level: no rising grade, no parked truck, no snow berm. The plan view on this page shows that arc for a concept gate.

If the drive climbs away from the gate, there are two honest fixes. Raise the hinge line and rake the bottom rail to follow the grade, or change to a gate that does not swing at all.

## Hinges, stops and latches

We use greaseable barrel hinges or sealed bearing hinges matched to the leaf. Double gates get a positive stop at center and a drop rod or automated lock so wind cannot rack the leaves. Manual gates get a latch you can work with a glove on.

## Automating a swing gate

Three operator styles cover nearly every swing gate.

- **Linear arm.** A screw-driven arm between post and leaf. Compact and common.
- **Articulated arm.** A folding arm that handles wide pillars and deep setbacks.
- **In-ground.** Hidden below the hinge. Clean, but it needs drainage that works in a thaw.

Whatever the style, an automated gate needs monitored safety devices. We cover that under [safety and compliance](/automation/safety-and-compliance/).

## When a swing gate is the wrong answer

Steep rise inside the gate. No room for the arc. A site where plowing the arc all winter is not going to happen. A very wide, solid panel in an open, windy location. In those cases we will recommend a [sliding gate](/gates/sliding-gates/) and show you why.
