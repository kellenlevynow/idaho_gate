## A gate shop is a fabrication shop

Building gates means cutting plate, coping tube, rolling arches, holding tolerances over twenty feet and putting down welds that take load and look good. The same equipment and the same hands build a great deal else. General fabrication is the rest of what comes through the door.

## The kind of work we take on

- **Brackets, mounts and frames** for equipment, signs, solar, decks and docks.
- **Trailer and implement work.** Racks, ramps, guards, hitches, repairs and reinforcements.
- **Building components.** Lintels, posts, base plates, embeds, stair parts.
- **Site metalwork.** Bollards, guard rails, enclosures, hatch frames, cattle guards.
- **Replacement parts.** For machines and structures that are out of production.
- **Cut and formed parts** for other trades and makers.

The drawing on this page is a concept bracket: a drilled base plate with a welded gusset. Small, but it shows how we think. Load path first, then welds, then how it gets installed.

## How a fabrication job runs

1. **Scope.** What it is, what it does, what it attaches to, how many, by when.
2. **Quote.** Material, labor, finish and any engineering, itemized.
3. **Drawing.** For anything beyond the simplest part, a shop drawing you approve.
4. **Fabrication.** Cut, fit, tack, check, weld, clean.
5. **Finish.** Raw, primed, painted, powder coated or galvanized.
6. **Delivery.** You pick up, we deliver, or we install.

More detail in [fabrication process](/company/fabrication-process/).

## Getting an accurate quote

Three things make a fabrication quote fast and accurate: dimensions, material and finish. If you know the load, the environment (indoors, outdoors, in water, near salt) and how it mounts, add those. Our guide to [working with a fabrication shop](/resources/working-with-a-fabrication-shop/) has a checklist.

## What we will tell you straight

If a part should be machined, cast or bought off the shelf, we will say so. If a design will distort when welded, or trap water, or cost twice what a small change would, we will show you the change. That is the value of talking to the people who will build it.
