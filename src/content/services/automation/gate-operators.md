## The gate comes first

An operator cannot fix a bad gate. If the leaf binds, sags or fights the wind, the operator will stall, reverse or burn out. So we start every automation job by making sure the gate swings or rolls freely by hand and stays where you leave it. Then we choose the machine.

## What decides the operator

- **Type of gate.** Swing or slide. Each has its own family of operators.
- **Leaf length and weight.** Manufacturers publish limits for both. We stay inside them with margin.
- **Wind area.** A solid gate loads the operator far more than open pickets.
- **Cycles per day.** A home might see ten. A community entrance might see a thousand.
- **Usage class.** UL 325 groups sites by who uses the gate, which sets safety requirements.
- **Power available.** Line voltage at the gate, a low-voltage run from the house, or none at all.
- **Geometry.** Pillar width, setback and swing angle decide which arm style will fit.

## Power and backup

Many residential operators run on low voltage with a battery that is kept charged by a transformer or a solar panel. That gives you operation through an outage for a limited number of cycles. Larger and busier gates run on line voltage, often with a separate backup. We size the supply for the operator and every accessory on it: lights, intercom, locks, heaters.

## What gets installed with it

An operator is never installed alone.

- **Entrapment protection.** Monitored photo eyes or edges, required by the safety standard.
- **Vehicle detection.** Loops or sensors for free exit and to hold the gate while a car is in the way.
- **Entry devices.** Keypad, remote, intercom or app. See [access control](/automation/access-control/).
- **Locks and stops.** A magnetic or electric lock where the gate needs positive hold.
- **Surge protection.** Rural power and lightning are hard on control boards.

## Setup and handover

After mounting, we set limits, force and timing, test every safety device, and walk you through the manual release, the controls and the app. You get the manuals and a short list of what to check each season. See the [maintenance checklist](/resources/gate-maintenance-checklist/).
