## What homeowners come to us for

Most residential projects start with one of four reasons.

- **Privacy.** Traffic on the road has grown, or the drive has become a turnaround.
- **Safety.** Children, dogs or livestock need to stay in. Strangers need to stay out.
- **Arrival.** The house is finished and the entrance is not.
- **A problem gate.** The one that came with the property sags, sticks or never worked.

Whatever the reason, the process is the same: visit, measure, draw, agree, build.

## Properties we see in North Idaho

**Lake and hillside lots.** Short, steep drives, tight setbacks from narrow roads, and neighbors close by. Sliding gates and compact swing gates earn their keep here. Aluminum makes sense near the water.

**Acreage on the prairie and in the timber.** Long drives, wide openings, wind, drifting snow and power a long way from the gate. Double swing or cantilever slide gates, often with solar.

**Subdivision and in-town lots.** HOA rules, sidewalks, sight lines and a need for the gate to suit the street. Shorter gates, pedestrian gates and courtyard entries.

## What the whole job includes

A gate is more than the leaf. For a typical home we handle:

1. Site measure and a concept drawing.
2. Posts and footings set below frost depth.
3. The gate, fabricated and finished in the shop.
4. Automation, safety devices and access control.
5. Pillars, lighting and address plate if you want them.
6. A walkthrough and a short maintenance routine.

You deal with one shop for all of it. Where another trade is needed, such as an electrician for a new circuit or a mason for stone, we coordinate it and tell you up front.
