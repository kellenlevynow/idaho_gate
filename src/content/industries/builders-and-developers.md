## What builders ask of a metal shop

We have been on the other side of enough schedules to know the list.

- **Read the drawings.** Structural and architectural, and notice when they disagree.
- **Ask early.** An RFI in week two is a favor. A surprise in week twenty is not.
- **Draw it.** Shop drawings that show what will actually arrive.
- **Hit the date.** Especially for anything cast in or holding up framing.
- **Fit the first time.** Field-verified dimensions on everything that meets existing work.

## Scope we cover

**Structure.** Columns, beams, moment frames, flitch plates, lintels, hold-downs and embeds, fabricated to the engineer's details.

**Stairs and rails.** Steel stringers, landings, interior guards, exterior deck and stair rail. Measured after framing so they fit the as-built, not the plan.

**Entry.** Driveway gates, pillars, operators and access control. Conduit and footings are best placed before final grade and paving.

**Features.** Canopies, trellises, fireplace surrounds, range hoods, screens and cut panels.

**Site.** Bollards, enclosures, grates and guard rail.

## Planning a gate into a build

Gates are often an afterthought on a custom home, added after the driveway is paved. Planning them into the site work saves money and mess.

1. **Locate the gate** on the site plan with its swing or slide clearance.
2. **Run conduit** for power and low voltage before the drive goes in.
3. **Set footings** with other concrete work.
4. **Coordinate pillars** with the mason and the electrician.
5. **Check fire access** requirements with the fire district during permitting.

Our guide to [permits, HOA rules and fire access](/resources/permits-hoa-and-fire-access/) covers the questions to ask.

## Developers and communities

A subdivision entrance is infrastructure. It needs to work for every resident, every delivery and every emergency vehicle from day one. We help with layout, operator and access selection, and the conduit plan, and we build the gates to suit the development's design standards.

## Working across the region

Building in Kootenai and Bonner counties, in Spokane County, or over the line in Montana brings different registration, permitting and tax rules. Tell us where the project is and we will confirm how we can serve it. See the [service area](/service-area/).
