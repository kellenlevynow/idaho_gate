## A region built on recreation

North Idaho and the country around it run on lakes, ski hills, golf, trails and the lodging that serves them. Those properties share a set of metalwork needs, and most of them are in plain view of paying guests.

## Entrances that fit the place

A lodge entrance, a golf community gate and a campground entry all do the same job in different clothes: mark the place, control vehicles and look like they belong. We design entrances around the property's architecture and name, with cut metal signage, lighting and gates sized for the traffic, including the occasional motorhome and delivery truck.

## On the water

The drawing on this page is a concept aluminum gangway. Marinas, resorts and lake communities need gangways, dock railings, ladders and stairs that tolerate water, sun and changing lake levels. Aluminum is the right material for most of it. See [aluminum fabrication](/fabrication/aluminum-fabrication/).

Shoreline and dock structures are often subject to state and local approval. Confirm what applies to your site before fabrication.

## Railings, terraces and overlooks

Outdoor dining decks, viewing platforms and stairs need guards that are safe and that do not block what people came to see. Cable infill and slim steel sections keep the view. Everything is built to the adopted commercial code.

## Access without friction

Guests should not have to think about the gate. Options include open hours with after-hours codes, codes issued with a booking, and separate staff and service credentials. Exit sensors let everyone leave freely. See [access control](/automation/access-control/).

## Durable by design

Hospitality metalwork is used by people who do not own it, in every season, often near water or de-icing salt.

- Finishes chosen for the exposure, usually galvanized, powder coated, or both.
- Hardware that tolerates heavy use.
- Details that drain and can be cleaned.
- Standard, replaceable parts where wear is expected.

Many properties schedule improvements for the shoulder seasons. Fabrication lead time means the conversation should start well before the quiet weeks. Get in touch through the [commercial quote path](/quote/?path=commercial) with your target dates.
