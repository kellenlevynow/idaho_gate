## Who we work with

- **Business owners** securing a yard, a lot or a storefront.
- **Property and facility managers** responsible for entrances, enclosures and repairs across one site or several.
- **HOAs and community managers** running a shared gate.
- **General contractors** buying gates and miscellaneous metals for a build.

## Typical commercial scope

**Access.** A vehicle gate with an operator sized for its duty cycle, monitored safety devices, vehicle detection and access control. Often a pedestrian gate beside it, because people should not walk through a vehicle gate.

**Protection.** Bollards at the operator, the pedestal, the gas meter and the building corner. Guard rail where vehicles and walls meet.

**Enclosures.** Refuse, mechanical and storage enclosures with gates that survive daily use.

**Life safety.** Guards, handrails and stair rails built to the adopted code and to accessibility requirements where they apply.

**Identity.** Cut metal signs and entrance features that suit the brand.

## How commercial jobs run

1. **Walk the site or review the plans.** We want to know traffic, vehicles, hours and who needs access.
2. **Scope and price.** A written scope with options where they exist.
3. **Submittals.** Shop drawings, product data and finish samples for approval.
4. **Fabricate and install.** On your schedule, with the entrance kept usable where possible.
5. **Commission.** Operators set up, safety devices tested, staff shown how it works.
6. **Service.** A maintenance schedule if you want one.

## Across the state line

Many North Idaho businesses also operate in Spokane County, and Washington has its own contractor registration and sales tax rules. If your project is in Washington, tell us at the start so the paperwork is handled properly. See our [service area](/service-area/).

## The standard we work to

Automated vehicular gates fall under UL 325 for the operator and ASTM F2200 for the gate. We specify to both and explain them in plain terms under [safety and compliance](/automation/safety-and-compliance/).
