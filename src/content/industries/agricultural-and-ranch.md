## Built for use

Rural gates and farm steel live a harder life than anything in town. They get bumped by implements, leaned on by animals, plowed against and left to the weather. We build for that with heavier sections, visible bracing, greaseable hardware and finishes that hold up.

## Entry gates for acreage

The questions that matter are practical.

- **What is the widest and tallest thing that uses this drive?**
- **Can a truck and trailer get fully off the road before the gate?**
- **Is there power at the gate, and if not, how far is it?**
- **Does the gate hold animals in?**
- **Where does the snow go?**

The answers decide width, type, automation and layout. Our page on [ranch and farm gates](/gates/ranch-and-farm-gates/) goes through each.

## Off-grid automation

Trenching power down a long drive is often the most expensive part of an automated rural gate. Solar avoids it. In this climate the system has to be sized for the short, dark days around the winter solstice. We size panels and batteries for that and keep standby loads low. See [solar gate operators](/automation/solar-gate-operators/).

## Entry arches

An arch with the ranch name is a tradition for good reason. It marks the place from a distance and it lasts. We set the posts like gate posts, span them with steel sized for wind and snow, and cut the sign panel from your artwork.

## Working fabrication

Between gate projects, the shop builds and fixes the steel that farms run on.

- Brackets, mounts and hitch parts.
- Racks, bunks and feeder frames.
- Guards and shields.
- Ramps and loading aids.
- Cattle guards and culvert grates.
- Repairs and reinforcements.

For repair work, a photo and a few measurements are usually enough to quote. Use the [fabrication path](/quote/?path=fabrication) in the quote form.

## Finishes for the long haul

Hot-dip galvanizing is hard to beat on working steel. Powder coat over galvanizing adds color where looks matter. Weathering steel needs no coating at all. We compare them in the [materials guide](/resources/materials-guide/).
