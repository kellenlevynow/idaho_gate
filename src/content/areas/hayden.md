## Entrances worth designing

Hayden runs from the commercial strip along the highway east to the lake and north into timbered acreage. The properties that call us most often are estates and larger residential lots, where the gate is expected to match the quality of the home.

**Lake estates.** Wooded, private and often seasonal. Typical requests are a double swing gate between stone pillars, lanterns, a cut-metal address plate and access that works for caretakers and guests. The drawing on this page is a concept in that spirit.

**Golf and planned communities.** Design review applies. Proportions, materials and colors are usually specified, and the gate has to sit well with its neighbors.

**Acreage north and west.** Toward Garwood and the prairie edge, parcels are larger and more open. Ranch-style and modern gates both show up here.

## Details that matter around the lake

- **Seasonal use.** A gate that sits closed for months needs a battery kept charged, a lock that holds, and a way for a caretaker to get in.
- **Privacy.** Solid or semi-solid infill gives privacy from the road. It also catches wind, so the structure steps up.
- **Lighting.** Low, warm and shielded. It marks the entrance without lighting up the neighbor's trees.
- **Finish.** Dark bronze and matte black suit timber and stone. Weathering steel works where runoff is managed.

## Planning notes

Hayden and Hayden Lake are separate cities with their own rules, and much of the lake shore is in unincorporated Kootenai County. The fire district serving the area may set requirements for gates across private roads and long drives. We check before design is final.
