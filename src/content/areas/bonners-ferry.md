## Built for the valley and the benches

Bonners Ferry sits on the Kootenai River with farmland on the valley floor and timbered benches above it. Canada is a short drive north. Properties are large, drives are long and neighbors are few.

That sets the brief for most gates here.

- **Wide.** For tractors, hay wagons, stock trailers and log trucks.
- **Strong.** Heavy tube or pipe, braced, on deep-set posts.
- **Simple.** Fewer parts to service a long way from town.
- **Honest.** A gate that looks like what it is.

## Entry arches and signs

The drawing on this page shows a concept ranch entry arch with a cut sign panel. Arches are a natural fit for this country. They mark the entrance from a distance on open ground and give the place a name. We draw the lettering and scenery ourselves and cut it on the CNC plasma table, so it is yours alone.

## Automation that fits off-grid life

If your home makes its own power, you already think in energy budgets. A solar gate works the same way. Movement costs little. Standby loads cost more over time. We keep the system separate from the house, size it for December, and avoid accessories that draw power around the clock. Details are on our [solar gate operators](/automation/solar-gate-operators/) page.

A good manual gate is also a perfectly sound choice, and we build those too.

## Farm fabrication

Between gates, we build and repair working steel: brackets, racks, guards, cattle guards and replacement parts. Send a photo and dimensions through the [fabrication quote path](/quote/?path=fabrication).

## Planning notes

- **Road approaches** onto county roads and state highways are handled by different agencies. Highways 95 and 2 belong to the state.
- **Border area.** Properties near the line can have additional considerations. Ask the county.
- **Stock.** Tell us if the gate holds animals. It changes the infill and how the gate behaves on a fault.
