## Gates for places people leave

West Bonner County is timber, river bottom and lake shore. Many properties are second homes or cabins used in summer and at the holidays. The gate has a different job here than it does in town.

- **Mark the property.** A proper gate at the road reads as private in a way a cable or a chain does not.
- **Stop vehicles.** Most opportunistic trouble arrives by truck.
- **Survive alone.** No one is there to notice a problem for weeks.
- **Let the right people in.** A caretaker, a plow driver, a neighbor checking the pipes.

## Keep it simple and strong

For seasonal properties we lean toward fewer parts. A single or double swing gate in heavy tube with rails and a brace, hung on steel posts in deep footings, with a lock box or a coded mechanical lock. The drawing on this page is a concept single ranch gate of that kind.

If you want to open it from the truck, a solar operator with a keypad and remotes adds convenience without a utility bill. We size the system for winter and keep accessories to the ones that draw little power. See [solar gate operators](/automation/solar-gate-operators/).

## Winter access

Decide early whether the drive will be plowed. If it will, the gate needs to open fully for the plow and stay clear of where snow gets pushed. If it will not, the gate should be able to sit closed under snow load until spring without damage.

## Around Priest River and Oldtown

In and near town, properties are year-round and closer to power. Standard automated driveway gates, pedestrian gates and fabrication work all apply. Oldtown sits on the state line with Newport, Washington. Projects on the Washington side follow Washington rules. See [Eastern Washington](/service-area/eastern-washington/).
