# Idaho Gate Co. business-line strategy

Prepared with the website build, November 2026.

This document recommends which business lines Idaho Gate Co. should present, in what order, and why. It separates two kinds of statement:

- **Sourced fact.** Something a public source says. Each has a link. Check it before relying on it, because figures and rules change.
- **Strategic hypothesis.** Our judgment about demand, margin, fit and timing. These are marked **(H)**. They are reasoned positions, not data. No market-size or revenue figures are given, because we do not have reliable ones and will not invent them.

The owner should test every hypothesis against real shop capacity, real quotes and the first six months of leads.

---

## 1. Summary recommendation

Present the company as **three divisions**, with custom gates clearly in front:

| # | Division | Role | Launch |
|---|---|---|---|
| 01 | Custom gates | The core. Highest intent, strongest brand, best lead history. | Now |
| 02 | Automation and access | Attached to nearly every gate. Also a standalone retrofit and repair business. | Now |
| 03 | Metal fabrication | Uses the same people and equipment. Smooths the calendar and opens builder and commercial accounts. | Now, with a focused menu |

Inside fabrication, lead with the lines closest to gate work (railings, screens and decorative panels, architectural metalwork), offer structural and miscellaneous steel to builders, and treat enclosures and guards as the commercial entry point. Hold production-style and certification-heavy work for later.

**Do not** bring general fencing back as a headline service **(H)**. It is a different business: volume-driven, price-compared and crew-intensive. It would dilute the positioning that makes a custom gate worth a premium. Fence tie-ins at a gate are handled inside gate projects.

The website already implements this structure. Divisions are data, so one can be added, renamed or switched off without redesign (see section 8).

## 2. What we know about the market (sourced)

**Kootenai County is growing.**
- The Census Bureau's 2024 estimate put Kootenai County at 188,323, up 9.9% from the April 2020 base of 171,362. ([Census QuickFacts](https://census.gov/quickfacts/fact/table/kootenaicountyidaho/PST045224))
- USAFacts reports about 191,900 residents in 2025, up 1.8% from 2024, with domestic migration as the main driver. ([USAFacts](https://usafacts.org/answers/is-the-population-growing-or-shrinking/county/kootenai-county-id/))
- Post Falls grew from 54,649 residents in April 2020 to 58,179 in July 2025, according to Census data reported locally. ([Coeur d'Alene Press, July 2026](https://cdapress.com/news/2026/jul/15/post-falls-among-nations-fastest-growing-cities-census-data-shows/))

**Spokane County is the largest nearby market.**
- Census QuickFacts lists 555,947 residents for July 2024, from a 2020 base of 539,346. ([Census QuickFacts](https://www.census.gov/quickfacts/fact/table/spokanecountywashington/PST045223))
- Washington's Office of Financial Management named Spokane one of the state's three fastest-growing counties in its April 2025 release, at 1.2%. ([Association of Washington Cities summary](https://wacities.org/advocacy/News/advocacy-news/2025/07/01/ofm-releases-new-population-numbers-for-the-state-and-cities))

**There are established local competitors.** A search of directories and listings turned up gate, fence and fabrication firms serving Coeur d'Alene and Spokane, including gate supply and installation companies in Post Falls and Coeur d'Alene, fence companies that also install operators, and family-run metal fabrication shops. ([BBB gates listings, Hayden](https://www.bbb.org/us/id/hayden/category/gates), [Spokane Journal of Business on a Mead gate maker](https://www.spokanejournal.com/articles/7105-the-gate-makers-of-mead)). We did not verify any competitor's capabilities, pricing or volume.

**Automated gates are governed by published safety standards.**
- UL 325 requires a vehicular gate operator to have at least two independent means of entrapment protection for each entrapment zone, and external devices must be monitored. ([DASMA TDS-353](https://www.dasma.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/TDS353-1.pdf), [DASMA gate systems safety brochure](https://www.dasma.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/10/GateSystemsSafetyBrochure.pdf))
- DASMA states that automated vehicular gates should be designed, built and installed to ASTM F2200. ([DASMA gate system safety](https://www.dasma.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/09/GateSystemSafety.pdf))

**Working across state lines has real requirements.**
- **Idaho:** contractors must register with the Division of Occupational and Professional Licenses for jobs of $2,000 or more. ([Idaho DOPL contractors FAQ](https://dopl.idaho.gov/con/con-faq/))
- **Washington:** contractors must register with the Department of Labor and Industries before working in the state. Since July 1, 2024, the required bond is $30,000 for general contractors and $15,000 for specialty contractors. ([WA L&I, register as a contractor](https://www.lni.wa.gov/licensing-permits/contractors/register-as-a-contractor/), [L&I news release on higher bonds](https://www.lni.wa.gov/news-events/article/24-13/))
- **Montana:** construction contractors with employees register with the Department of Labor and Industry. ([Montana DLI construction contractor registration](https://erd.dli.mt.gov/work-comp-regulations/montana-contractor/construction-contractor-registration))
- **Sales tax differs.** Idaho treats contractors as the consumers of materials they install in real property. ([Idaho State Tax Commission, contractors](https://tax.idaho.gov/contractors)) Washington and Montana apply their own rules. Get advice from a tax professional before quoting out of state.

**Fire access rules affect gate design.** Model fire code says a security gate across a fire apparatus access road must be approved by the fire chief, and local districts commonly require emergency key access for powered gates. Local adoption and amendments control. ([Example: Lower Valley Fire District gate requirements, Idaho](https://lowervalleyfire.specialdistrict.org/files/7cb79315c/Requirements%2Bfor%2BSecurity%2BGates+%281%29.pdf))

## 3. How each line was judged

Each candidate was scored on the criteria in the brief. Ratings are **hypotheses (H)**, on a High / Medium / Low scale, from the point of view of a small custom fabrication shop that already builds gates.

| Criterion | What "High" means |
|---|---|
| Local demand | Many buyers in the service area who need it and search for it |
| Competition | Low score is better: few capable local providers |
| Revenue per job | Large ticket size |
| Margin | Custom, design-led, hard to price-shop |
| Equipment fit | Uses equipment a gate shop already has |
| Labor fit | Uses the same skills and crew |
| Complexity | Low score is better: few new processes, licenses or risks |
| Cross-sell | Naturally sold to the same customer |
| Recurring work | Repeat customers or service revenue |
| Travels across state lines | Can be fabricated in the shop and delivered |

## 4. Division 01: custom gates (launch now)

### Lines
Driveway gates, estate and architectural entrance gates, modern gates, swing gates, sliding and cantilever gates, ranch and farm gates, pedestrian and garden gates, commercial and security gates, pillars and columns, address plates and lighting, repairs and upgrades.

### Why it makes sense
- The previous site generated multiple qualified leads a day on gate work. That is the proven demand signal for this business. *(Owner's report, from the build brief.)*
- **(H)** A custom gate is a considered purchase where design and trust matter more than the lowest bid, which supports margin.
- **(H)** Growth in Kootenai County means new homes on acreage and in planned communities, both of which generate gate demand. The population growth is sourced above. The link to gate demand is our inference.

### Who buys
Homeowners on acreage, lake and hillside lots. Owners of seasonal properties. Ranch and farm owners. HOAs and developers. Businesses with yards and lots.

### Equipment and capability
Saw, CNC plasma table, tube roller, welding (MIG, TIG for aluminum), fixtures and flat tables, lifting equipment, a truck and trailer, concrete and post-setting tools, access to powder coating and galvanizing. *(Confirm the actual equipment list. See TODO-CONTENT.md.)*

### Commercial potential (H)
| Demand | Competition | Revenue/job | Margin | Complexity | Cross-sell | Recurring |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| High | Medium | High | High | Medium | High | Low to medium |

### Sub-line notes
- **Estate gates** carry the largest tickets and the most design work. They are the brand-builders. Lead with them visually.
- **Cantilever slide gates** suit the region's snow and slope. Positioning the company as the shop that understands winter is a real differentiator **(H)**.
- **Ranch gates and entry arches** travel well to Boundary County and Western Montana as fabricate-and-deliver jobs **(H)**.
- **Commercial gates** are a different sale (submittals, duty-rated operators, liability). Offer them, but route enquiries separately. The quote form does this.
- **Repairs and upgrades** bring recurring revenue and are often the first contact that leads to a replacement gate **(H)**. They need a fast response to be worth advertising.

## 5. Division 02: automation and access (launch now)

### Lines
Gate operators, access control (keypads, intercoms, app control), solar gate operators, safety devices and compliance, retrofits.

### Why it makes sense
- Nearly every driveway gate enquiry includes automation. Selling the gate without it leaves revenue and control of the outcome to someone else **(H)**.
- Safety standards have tightened (sourced above). **(H)** Many older gates in the region lack monitored safety devices, which creates upgrade work and a credible educational angle.
- **(H)** Solar is a strong fit for a region with long rural driveways and off-grid properties.

### Who buys
Every gate customer. Owners of existing manual gates. Property managers and HOAs. Owners of failing or outdated operators.

### Equipment and capability
Low: hand tools, meters, trenching or boring access. The requirement is knowledge: operator selection, UL 325 device placement, low-voltage wiring, programming. New circuits need a licensed electrician. Manufacturer dealer or installer status may be required for some product lines. *(Confirm brands and any certifications.)*

### Commercial potential (H)
| Demand | Competition | Revenue/job | Margin | Complexity | Cross-sell | Recurring |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| High | Medium | Medium | Medium to high | Medium | Very high | Medium to high |

### Risk to manage
Automation carries the most liability of anything the company does. The website's content is deliberately strict about safety devices. The business should be equally strict in practice, and insurance should reflect automated gate work.

## 6. Division 03: metal fabrication (launch now, focused)

General fabrication is included as a business line, as the brief requires. The question is which parts of it to lead with.

### 6.1 Launch now

**Railings and handrails**
- *Why:* **(H)** High, steady demand from decks, stairs and hillside lots across lake country. Code-driven, so buyers need it done properly.
- *Buyer:* homeowners, builders, commercial property owners.
- *Equipment:* already in the shop. Needs careful site measure and code knowledge.
- *Fit with gates:* same customer, same finish, often the same visit.
- *Potential (H):* Demand High, Margin Medium to high, Complexity Low to medium.

**Privacy screens and decorative panels**
- *Why:* **(H)** Shows off the CNC table and in-house drawing, which catalog competitors cannot match. Light, design-led and easy to deliver.
- *Buyer:* homeowners, designers, businesses wanting signage.
- *Equipment:* CNC plasma, finishing.
- *Fit with gates:* the same panels go into gates. Address plates and ranch signs are natural add-ons.
- *Potential (H):* Demand Medium, Margin High, Complexity Low. Travels well.

**Architectural metalwork**
- *Why:* **(H)** Custom homes and resort properties in the region use exposed steel, canopies and entry features. Low volume, high value, strong portfolio pieces.
- *Buyer:* architects, custom builders, high-end homeowners, hospitality.
- *Equipment:* in the shop. Engineering by a licensed engineer where structural.
- *Potential (H):* Demand Medium, Margin High, Complexity Medium to high.

**General fabrication and repair**
- *Why:* **(H)** Fills gaps in the schedule, serves the agricultural base, and creates local goodwill. Every small job is a future gate referral.
- *Buyer:* farms, ranches, trades, homeowners, small businesses.
- *Equipment:* in the shop.
- *Potential (H):* Demand High, Margin Medium, Complexity Low. Watch that small jobs do not crowd out gates in peak season.

**Custom and structural steel for builders**
- *Why:* **(H)** Custom home builders need a responsive source for one-off beams, posts, embeds and frames. It opens builder relationships that also produce gate and railing work.
- *Buyer:* custom home builders and general contractors.
- *Equipment:* in the shop for residential and light commercial scale.
- *Constraint:* some specifications require a certified fabricator. Be explicit about what the shop holds. The site says "ask us about current certifications" until confirmed.
- *Potential (H):* Demand Medium, Margin Medium, Complexity Medium.

**Aluminum fabrication**
- *Why:* **(H)** The region's lakes create steady demand for gangways, dock rail and ladders. Aluminum also improves the gate offer (light leaves, solar gates).
- *Buyer:* lakefront owners, marinas, resorts.
- *Equipment:* TIG or pulse MIG capability and aluminum-specific practice. *(Confirm.)*
- *Potential (H):* Demand Medium, seasonal. Margin Medium to high. Complexity Medium.

**Enclosures, guards and bollards**
- *Why:* **(H)** The easiest entry into commercial accounts. Refuse enclosures and bollards appear on nearly every commercial site plan, are often bought late, and are frequently built badly.
- *Buyer:* general contractors, property managers, facility managers.
- *Equipment:* in the shop.
- *Potential (H):* Demand Medium to high in Spokane and along the I-90 corridor. Margin Medium. Complexity Low. Good recurring potential with contractors.

### 6.2 Launch later, once capacity and paperwork support it

| Line | Why wait (H) |
|---|---|
| Commercial perimeter and high-security systems | Needs bonding capacity, submittal discipline and specialist partners. Start with individual commercial gates. |
| Larger structural steel and erection | Needs certifications, cranes and a different risk profile. |
| Scheduled maintenance contracts | Valuable recurring revenue, but only once there is service capacity to honor response times. Offer informally first. |
| Dock and waterfront as a named sub-brand | Wait for a season of real projects and photographs. |
| Custom metal furniture and fire features as product lines | Good portfolio pieces, but a product business needs inventory and retail marketing. Keep as "specialty projects". |
| Washington as a core market | Requires Washington registration, bond and tax setup (sourced above). Worth doing deliberately, not incidentally. |

### 6.3 Do not pursue (H)
- **General fencing by the mile.** Different economics and positioning. It was on the old site and is intentionally not on this one.
- **Production machining, casting, pressure vessels, ductwork.** Different equipment and certifications.
- **Mobile welding as an advertised service.** It invites low-value, urgent, travel-heavy calls. Take it for existing customers.

### 6.4 Additional lines worth considering (H)
These were not in the brief. We think they fit the region.

1. **Ranch entry arches and cut-metal signs** as a named product. Low competition for well-drawn work, high visibility, travels across state lines. Already featured on the site.
2. **Snow-country retrofits.** Converting failing swing or tracked gates to cantilever slides, and winterizing operators. A specific, searchable problem in this climate.
3. **HOA and community entrance upgrades.** Older shared gates that need current safety devices and modern access control. One sale, long relationship.
4. **Builder gate-ready packages.** Conduit, footings and posts installed during site work for homes that will add a gate later. Low cost to the builder, and it locks in the future gate.

## 7. Geographic strategy

| Tier | Area | Stance |
|---|---|---|
| Core | Kootenai County: Coeur d'Alene, Post Falls, Hayden, Rathdrum, Spirit Lake, Athol | Full service, fastest response |
| Extended | Bonner, Boundary, Shoshone and Benewah counties. Spokane County, WA | Regular routes, free site visits, scheduled by trip |
| Project | Farther into Eastern Washington. Sanders, Lincoln and Mineral counties, MT | Job by job. Often fabricate and deliver |

**(H)** This order follows drive time and regulatory friction. Idaho work needs only Idaho registration. Washington and Montana each add registration, insurance and tax setup. Spokane County is the largest population within reach (sourced above) and is the logical first expansion, but only once Washington registration is in place.

The website states these tiers openly, and the Washington and Montana pages say plainly that work there is by project and subject to that state's rules. **The owner must confirm every tier and every registration before launch.**

## 8. How the website supports this

- **Divisions are data.** `src/data/nav.ts` lists the divisions. Each has a slug, name, summary, groups and a default enquiry path. Setting `enabled: false` removes a division from navigation, hubs, footer and sitemap.
- **Services are files.** Each service is a Markdown file in `src/content/services/<division>/`. Adding a line means adding a file.
- **Four enquiry paths.** Custom gate, fabrication, commercial, and repair or upgrade. Each asks its own questions and can route to its own inbox.
- **Lead scoring.** Every lead is scored on timeline, automation, size, budget and urgency so hot leads are called first.
- **Service attribution.** The site records which service page a visitor viewed last and sends it with the lead, so you can see which lines actually produce enquiries.

That last point is how to test the hypotheses in this document. After 90 days, compare leads and closed jobs by service page and by enquiry path, and prune or promote lines accordingly.

## 9. Assumptions to confirm

1. The shop owns or has reliable access to a CNC plasma table, tube rolling, and MIG and TIG welding.
2. Powder coating and galvanizing are available regionally with workable lead times.
3. The company holds, or will obtain, Idaho contractor registration, and will obtain Washington and Montana registration before taking work there.
4. Automation brands and any dealer or installer status (the previous site named LiftMaster and DoorKing).
5. Insurance covers automated gate installation and fabrication for third parties.
6. Crew capacity can serve the extended tier on a regular route without hurting core response time.
7. The owner wants builder and commercial work, which brings longer payment terms and more paperwork.
